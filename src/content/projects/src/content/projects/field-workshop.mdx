Practical, on-site experience that runs alongside the research and fabrication work.

## Construction supervision

As a Lieutenant with the Defense Installations Agency (Ministry of National Defense),
supervised construction of several projects at Air Force facilities in Cheongju,
Korea, 2020–2023: a church (2,800 m²), an engineering office and working station
(1,600 m²), and a gymnasium (599 m²), designed by SIMYUNG Architects and TOPEC
Engineering.

## International workshops

**Clinic of Care in Indonesia: Adaptive Reuse** — Holcim Foundation, 2024, Yogyakarta,
Indonesia. Co-workshop member.

**ACAU (Asian Coalition for Architecture & Urbanism), Collective City** — 2019, Ho Chi
Minh City. Co-workshop member, tri-party student collaboration.
