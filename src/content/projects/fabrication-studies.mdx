A running series of shorter fabrication studies, each testing a different material
or tooling constraint rather than a single continuous project. Click each item for
more detail.

<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Column <span class="spec-credit">AIK 2023 · Jeongseon</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

Robot-arm extrusion tests exploring the print head's size limitations and
suitable print geometry, using a biodegradable formwork printed from seaweed-waste
bioplastic (FGF). The formwork was printed in three plastic-combination units of
300mm, under the machine's printing limit, then used to cast UHPC — producing a
900mm column referencing a traditional wooden pillar. Once cast, the printed
formwork itself biodegrades back into the soil. With Yousub Bang and Prof. Jungwon
Yoon.

</div>
</details>

<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Shellfoam <span class="spec-credit">Workshop · 2023, Seoul</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

A robot-arm knife-cutting workshop exploring shellfoam as a lightweight, carvable
formwork material — testing how far a subtractive robotic process can shape a
foam block before it's used as formwork or finish surface. With Prof. Junhee Cho
and UOS students.

</div>
</details>

<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Air <span class="spec-credit">Workshop · 2020, Seoul</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

A pneumatic, air-expansion fabrication workshop testing inflatable vinyl forms as
a lightweight alternative to rigid formwork — expanding sealed pockets under
controlled air pressure to generate form. With Prof. Jie-Eun Hwang and UOS
students.

</div>
</details>

<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Chair <span class="spec-credit">Lab research · 2023, Seoul</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

A lab-based furniture study applying the group's ribbon-extrusion fabrication
method at product scale, testing continuous-strip forming for seating. With
Yousub Bang.

</div>
</details>

<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Concrete <span class="spec-credit">Workshop · 2018, Seoul</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

An early UHPC (ultra-high-performance concrete) workshop exploring transparent,
thin-shell concrete casting — an entry point into the formwork and material
logic that later fed into the Column study. With Prof. Jie-Eun Hwang and UOS
students.

</div>
</details>
