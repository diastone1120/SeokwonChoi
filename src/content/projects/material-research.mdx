<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Algae (seaweed) <span class="spec-credit">AIK 2023 · Jeongseon</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

Seaweed-waste-derived bioplastic (blended with PBAT and PLA compounds — BG1000,
850D, NK100, 3100HP) was tested across more than a dozen mixing ratios for FGF
printability, evaluated against three recurring failure modes: inability to
stack, inconsistent material supply, and shrinking. Presented at the Autumn
Conference of AIK, 2023, Jeongseon, as "A Study on the Architectural Prototyping
of Seaweed Plastic Compound by FGF."

<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Shape-memory polymer (SMP) <span class="spec-credit">AIK 2018 · CAADRIA 2020</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

3D-printed SMP prototypes — lattice, blossom, and swirl disc forms — were
developed into hinge-actuator models that fold under an applied load and restore
their shape when heated to 55°C. Presented at the Autumn Conference of AIK,
2018, Pyeongchang, and at CAADRIA 2020, Bangkok, as "Digital Design and Additive
Manufacturing of SMP Prototypes."
