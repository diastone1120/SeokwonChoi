<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Earth <span class="spec-credit">ISAIA 2024 · Kyoto</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

Lime-earth block mixtures were tested with natural fibers (rice husk, kenaf,
coconut fiber, wheat straw, agar, and others) across varying earth-lime-water
ratios, evaluated for crack resistance, surface texture, and color stability over
a 7-day cure. Blocks were compression-tested and cast into a seashell-lime bench,
built with an international team at Naturalwangju Folly 5. Explored first through
that workshop, then developed further into the Master's thesis presented at the
14th ISAIA 2024 Conference in Kyoto.

<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Algae (seaweed) <span class="spec-credit">AIK 2023 · Jeongseon</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

Seaweed-waste-derived bioplastic (blended with PBAT and PLA compounds — BG1000,
850D, NK100, 3100HP) was tested across more than a dozen mixing ratios for FGF
printability, evaluated against three recurring failure modes: inability to
stack, inconsistent material supply, and shrinking. Print temperature was tuned
per blend between roughly 150–195°C to find a stable, printable compound.
Presented at the Autumn Conference of AIK, 2023, Jeongseon, as "A Study on the
Architectural Prototyping of Seaweed Plastic Compound by FGF."

<FigureCard src="/images/algae-blending.jpg" num="02" caption="Bioplastic blending process and print-result comparison across material ratios" />

</div>
</details>

<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Shape-memory polymer (SMP) <span class="spec-credit">AIK 2018 · CAADRIA 2020</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

3D-printed SMP prototypes — lattice, blossom, and swirl disc forms — were
developed into hinge-actuator models that fold under an applied load and restore
their shape when heated to 55°C. Infill wall thickness was tested from 2mm to
9mm to study its effect on shape-recovery behavior, alongside spiral and radial
actuator variants. Presented at the Autumn Conference of AIK, 2018, Pyeongchang,
and at CAADRIA 2020, Bangkok, as "Digital Design and Additive Manufacturing of
SMP Prototypes."
