A materials-first thread running alongside the larger projects — testing
individual material systems before they're combined into full-scale prototypes.
Click each item for more detail.

<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Earth <span class="spec-credit">ISAIA 2024 · Kyoto</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

Lime-earth mixtures tested across varying earth type, lime ratio, and fiber
reinforcement, evaluated for crack resistance and moisture behavior over multiple
curing periods. Explored first through the Gwangju Folly 5 workshop, then
developed further into the Master's thesis presented at the 14th ISAIA 2024
Conference in Kyoto.

</div>
</details>

<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Algae (seaweed) <span class="spec-credit">AIK 2023 · Jeongseon</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

Seaweed-waste-derived bioplastic compounds (blended with PBAT/PLA) tested for FGF
printability — comparing blend ratios and print temperatures for stacking
stability, supply consistency, and shrinkage. Presented at the Autumn Conference
of AIK, 2023, Jeongseon, as "A Study on the Architectural Prototyping of Seaweed
Plastic Compound by FGF."

</div>
</details>

<details class="spec-item">
<summary>Shape-memory polymer (SMP) <span class="spec-credit">AIK 2018 · CAADRIA 2020</span></summary>
<div class="spec-body">

3D-printed SMP prototypes studied for shape-recovery behavior across varying
infill densities and lattice patterns, including hinge-actuator forms that
unfold when heated. Presented at the Autumn Conference of AIK, 2018, Pyeongchang,
and at CAADRIA 2020, Bangkok, as "Digital Design and Additive Manufacturing of
SMP Prototypes."

</div>
</details>
