## Concept

A space-training center inserted into a former military installation, exploring
the space left behind a boundary wall that once separated a closed military site
from the surrounding neighborhood. Rather than erasing that division, the design
treats it as spatial material — folding public program through, along, and behind
the old boundary.

## Program

The training center offers a full simulated-astronaut experience: G-load /
gravity acceleration and zero-gravity training, a simulated starship and lunar
surface, dark-room and mental / physical training zones, alongside public-facing
exhibition rooms, a cafeteria, and reception — layering specialized training
space with an openly public exhibition sequence.
