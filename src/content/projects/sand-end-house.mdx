A competition entry rethinking housing for desert conditions using what's already
on site: sand.

## Concept

Rather than importing conventional building materials, the proposal uses local sand
bound through bacterial biocementation — a process where bacteria trigger mineral
precipitation to bind loose sand into a solid, load-bearing material. The approach
draws on vernacular desert-building logic while introducing a biologically-driven,
low-carbon binding method in place of cement.

## Keywords

Housing, local material, vernacular construction, sand, bacteria.
