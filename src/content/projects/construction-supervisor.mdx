three projects at Air Force facilities in Cheongju, designed by SIMYUNG Architects
and TOPEC Engineering:

- **Catholic Church & Christian Church** — 2,800 m², 2021.10–2023.03
- **Office & Working Station** — 1,600 m², 2021.02–2022.03
- **Gymnasium** — 599 m², 2020.10–2021.08
