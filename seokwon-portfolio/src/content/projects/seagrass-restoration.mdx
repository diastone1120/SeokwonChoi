## Context

Seagrass meadows are fragile coastal ecosystems that stabilize seabeds and support
marine biodiversity. Tetrapod units are commonly used to anchor and protect areas
undergoing restoration — this project reimagines them as a digitally fabricated,
bio-based prosthesis rather than plain concrete.

## Method

Target seabed surfaces were 3D-scanned and translated into printable geometry through
three fabrication methods, ranging from simple planar stacking to fully non-planar,
robotically controlled direct printing with infinite stacking. Seaweed-derived
bioplastic (PBAT/PLA compounds) was extruded through a pellet extruder mounted on an
ABB IRB1200 six-axis robot arm.

## Outcome

Presented at DigitalFUTURES 2024 in Shanghai as the second-authored paper, "Enhancing
Seagrass Habitat Restoration: 3D Scanning and FGF for Tetrapod Prosthesis."
