A materials-first thread running alongside the larger projects — testing individual
material systems before they're combined into full-scale prototypes.

## Earth

Explored through the Gwangju Folly 5 workshop and the Master's thesis work later
presented at the 14th ISAIA 2024 Conference in Kyoto.

## Algae (seaweed)

Seaweed-derived bioplastic compounds tested for FGF printability. Presented at the
Autumn Conference of AIK, 2023, Jeongseon, as "A Study on the Architectural
Prototyping of Seaweed Plastic Compound by FGF."

## Shape-memory polymer (SMP)

3D-printed SMP prototypes studied for shape-recovery behavior at varying infill
densities. Presented at the Autumn Conference of AIK, 2018, Pyeongchang, and at
CAADRIA 2020, Bangkok, as "Digital Design and Additive Manufacturing of SMP
Prototypes."
