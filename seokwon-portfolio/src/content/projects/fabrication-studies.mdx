A running series of shorter fabrication studies, each testing a different material
or tooling constraint rather than a single continuous project.

## Column

Robot-arm extrusion tests exploring size limitations and suitable print geometry.
Presented at the Autumn Conference of AIK, 2023, Jeongseon (with Yousub Bang and
Prof. Jungwon Yoon).

## Shellfoam

A robot-arm knife-cutting workshop exploring shellfoam as a lightweight, carvable
formwork material. 2023, Seoul, with Prof. Junhee Cho and UOS students.

## Air

A pneumatic, air-expansion fabrication workshop. 2020, Seoul, with Prof. Jie-Eun
Hwang and UOS students.

## Chair

A lab-based furniture study applying the group's fabrication methods at product
scale. 2023, Seoul, with Yousub Bang.

## Concrete

An early UHPC (ultra-high-performance concrete) workshop exploring transparent,
thin-shell concrete casting. 2018, Seoul, with Prof. Jie-Eun Hwang and UOS students.
